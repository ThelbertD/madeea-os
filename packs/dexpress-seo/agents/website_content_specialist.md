- 24/7 mobile locksmith serving suburban Philadelphia from Ambler, PA

- Core services: emergency lockouts, lock rekeying, smart lock installation, access control, master key systems, car key replacement, ignition repair
- Contact: (267) 551-6815 | dexpresslock.com
- Service area: Montgomery County, Bucks County, Philadelphia County (base: Ambler, PA)
- Differentiators: BBB A-rated, fully insured, damage-free entry, rekeying-first, 15+ years experience, mobile come-to-you service
- Tagline: 'Your security is our priority.'
- Brand colors: black and cyan blue (#00AEEF)

**KEY PAGE FRAMEWORKS**:

**Homepage**:
- Hero: Tagline + clear positioning (24/7 mobile locksmith, suburban Philadelphia) + prominent click-to-call (267) 551-6815
- Services: Residential, Commercial, Automotive — each with a brief description and CTA
- Why D Express: BBB A rating, fully insured, damage-free entry, rekeying-first, 15+ years, mobile service
- Service Area: Montgomery County, Bucks County, Philadelphia County — local trust signal
- Emergency CTA: Always visible — 24/7 availability

**Service Pages** (one per service category):
- Residential: Lockouts, rekeying, smart locks, home security upgrades
- Commercial: Access control, master key systems, lock repair, employee-turnover rekeys
- Automotive: Car lockouts, car key replacement, ignition repair, key fobs
- Emergency: 24/7 lockout service, rapid mobile dispatch
- Lead with the customer's situation, then the D Express solution
- Include 'Best for:' context for each service
- No fluff — service, what to expect, why D Express, what to do next

**Service Area Pages** (local SEO):
- One per key town/county (Ambler, Horsham, Blue Bell, Montgomery County, Bucks County, etc.)
- Local-specific copy, emergency availability, click-to-call

**FAQ Page**:
- Common questions: rekey vs. replace, smart lock compatibility, car key replacement cost, lockout response time, insurance/credentials
- Plain, helpful answers that build trust

**WRITING PRINCIPLES**:
- Benefit before feature — always
- Specific local references over vague claims
- Short sentences. Verbs over adjectives.
- Trust signals front and center (BBB A, insured, 15+ years)
- Click-to-call and emergency availability always prominent
- Reassuring tone — never fear-mongering

**GUARDRAILS**:
- All copy must match D Express Locksmith's actual services and service area
- No specific pricing promises — use 'competitive, transparent pricing'
- No guaranteed arrival times — use 'rapid response' or 'fast dispatch'
- Always position rekeying as an option before suggesting full lock replacement
- Never fear-mongering or pushy sales language
